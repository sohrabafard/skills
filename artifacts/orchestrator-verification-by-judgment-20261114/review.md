1. skills/sohrab/alaa-workflow/references/review-mode.md:51 | major | Outside the floors, a disputed item that one check does not settle forces a fresh refuter dispatch, even when the parent did the review itself. Design (Findings): "Outside the floors the decision is the lead's, and it records it." Replace the sentences from "Outside the floors, one discriminating check may settle it." through "This applies also when the parent did the review itself." with: "Outside the floors, the parent settles it with one discriminating check, or decides it and records the reason. On a floor surface, dispatch one fresh read-only refuter per lens that has disputed items, not one per item, because the parent cannot overrule the floor gate." Keep the rest of the paragraph from "Use a fresh instance of the role that owns the lens".
2. skills/sohrab/alaa-cc-orchestrator/references/routing-matrix.md:170 | major | "Schedule independent judgment at the declared boundary." This forces the contract reviewer for every contract shape change, including additive ones. That contradicts verification-and-gates.md:20 ("Elsewhere a holding trigger ... dispatches its specialist only with the reason the decision states") and routing-matrix.md:155. The floor covers only a breaking change. Replace with: "Schedule the judgment that the verification decision or the breaking-contract floor in `verification-and-gates.md` calls for at the declared boundary."
3. skills/sohrab/alaa-cc-orchestrator/references/verification-and-gates.md:52 (with :56 and :204) | major | A floor fix can be approved by its own author. Who fixes lets the lead make a small correction with no floor carve-out. After a fix treats "touched a floor surface" as a reason that permits a review, not one that requires it. Final success criteria accept a floor finding as "resolved" without naming who judges that. So the lead can fix an auth finding, run the failing check and accept it itself, against "the author never approves its own work". At :52, replace "A further review needs a stated reason: the fix changed the design, touched a floor surface, or the first review could not see what it needed." with: "A fix that touches a floor surface returns to that floor's gate, because its author cannot approve it; the gate judges the fix, not the whole change again. Any other further review needs a stated reason: the fix changed the design, or the first review could not see what it needed." At :204, replace "each blocking floor-gate finding is resolved or explicitly accepted by the user" with "each blocking floor-gate finding is confirmed fixed by that gate or explicitly accepted by the user".
4. verification-and-gates.md:38 | minor | No gate is named for a floor surface that no specialist trigger covers. Replace "`alaa-reviewer` is the gate when the user asks for a review and no specialist fits." with "`alaa-reviewer` is the gate on a floor surface that no specialist trigger covers, including a review the user asks for." This adds no specialist trigger.
5. verification-and-gates.md:95-96; skills/sohrab/alaa-cc-orchestrator/SKILL.md:142 | minor | "after the final review accepts it" and "after that review accepts it" make the merge sound conditional on a review.
